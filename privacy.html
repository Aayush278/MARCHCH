Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

MARCSFUNDHANG is committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your data.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you register as an investor:

  • Full name and contact information (email, phone number)
  • Investment amount and portfolio data
  • Transaction history
  • Login credentials (password is encrypted and never stored in plain text)

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used exclusively to:

  • Manage your investment portfolio
  • Provide you access to your portfolio dashboard
  • Send important announcements related to your investments
  • Maintain accurate records of your transactions

3. Data Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase cloud infrastructure with industry-standard encryption. Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text. Access to your data is strictly limited to you and authorized MARCSFUNDHANG administrators.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with any third parties. Your data is never used for advertising or marketing purposes beyond MARCSFUNDHANG communications.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to your personal data, request corrections, or request deletion of your account. Please contact us directly to exercise these rights.

6. Contact

For any privacy-related queries, please contact MARCSFUNDHANG through the contact information on our website.
